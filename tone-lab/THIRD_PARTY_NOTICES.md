# Vocabulary data

HSK 2.0 Level 4 headwords and Mandarin readings adapted from [Complete HSK Vocabulary](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary). Readings were selected for the Korean gloss shown; several ambiguous first-entry readings were corrected. Korean glosses are original additions. This is an independent study aid, not an official HSK product.

## Audio

The Mandarin word and tone examples are synthesized speech (zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural). Audio timing metadata is stored in audio.mjs. The following single-character heteronyms use a matching Mandarin homophone to make the intended reading unambiguous: 弹→谈 (tán), 行→型 (xíng), 假→甲 (jiǎ), 干→甘 (gān). They are not recordings by a human voice actor.
